BADMINTON

A fast racquet sport played by two players (singles) or four players (doubles) using a lightweight racquet and a shuttlecock. Players score points by hitting the shuttlecock over a net so it lands in the other side. Matches use rally scoring, playing best of three games to 21 points. Rules and ScoringScoring: Best of three games. First to 21 points wins a game. Must win by two points or first to reach 30 points.Serving: Must hit the shuttlecock below the waist and diagonally across the net.Rally: Every error or grounded shuttlecock gives a point to the opponent.Court and EquipmentCourt Size: 13.4 meters (44 feet) long. Width is 5.18 meters (17 feet) for singles and 6.1 meters (20 feet) for doubles.
